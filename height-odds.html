How many men are 6 feet tall?

Height odds for men and women · Last updated: September 19, 2026

About 1 in 6 American men is 6 feet or taller, according to the height model our calculator uses. Set a 6-foot minimum and you’ve ruled out roughly 84% of men before any other standard comes into it.

What stands out

  • 5′9″ is the middle. Half of men are 5′9″ or taller and half are shorter, so any minimum above 5′9″ rules out most men.
  • From 6 feet up, each inch roughly halves the pool. 16% of men are 6′0″ or taller, 9.1% are 6′1″ or taller, 4.8% are 6′2″ and 2.3% are 6′3″.
  • 6′4″ is about 1 man in 100.
  • For women, 5′8″ is already uncommon. About 1 woman in 17 is 5′8″ or taller, and a 6-foot woman is closer to 1 in 750.

How these numbers are worked out

Adult height follows a bell curve closely, so the calculator models it as one: an average of 5′9″ for men with a standard deviation of 3 inches, and about 5′3½″ for women with a standard deviation of 2.8 inches, based on CDC/NCHS body-measurement data (NHANES).

These are estimates from a statistical model, not a head count. They’re most accurate near the average and least precise at the extremes, so treat the very tall end of the table as a ballpark rather than an exact figure.
