Refund Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Digital delivery

The Global Dream Partner Report is a digital product that unlocks immediately after payment. Because of that instant access, we generally don't offer refunds simply for a change of mind once the report has been unlocked and viewed.

When we will refund you

  • You were charged but never actually got access to your report due to a technical problem on our end.
  • You were charged more than once for the same report by mistake.
  • Something else went genuinely wrong with your purchase.

If any of these happen, email outofpocketinterviews@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout, and we'll sort it out — refunds are issued back to your original payment method through Stripe.

Not sure if you qualify?

Reach out anyway. We'd rather hear from you and make it right than have you feel stuck — every request is reviewed individually.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent version.
