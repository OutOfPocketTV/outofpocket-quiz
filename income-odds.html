How many people earn $100k a year?

Income odds for men and women · Last updated: September 19, 2026

About 1 in 5 American men and 1 in 7 women earn $100,000 or more a year, according to the income model our calculator uses. Raise that bar to $200,000 and the pool of men shrinks by about two-thirds.

What stands out

  • The typical man earns about $45,000 and the typical woman about $33,000. Those are medians (half earn more, half earn less), so any minimum above them rules out most people.
  • $100,000 is roughly the top fifth of men.
  • Each step up costs more than it looks. Going from $100,000 to $150,000 roughly halves the share of men who qualify, and at $250,000 it’s about 1 in 25.
  • Seven-figure earners are about 1 man in 1,000.

How these numbers are worked out

Incomes aren’t spread out evenly. Lots of people earn modest amounts and a few earn a great deal, so the calculator models them with a log-normal curve, the standard shape for income. It’s fitted to median personal income from the U.S. Census Bureau’s American Community Survey ($45,000 for men, $33,000 for women) and calibrated so the very top lands near real top-earner rates.

This is personal income before tax, not household income, and it comes from a model rather than a head count, so it is most reliable around the middle and least precise at the very top. It also covers all adults, so it doesn’t adjust for age: people in their twenties typically earn less than this curve suggests, and people in their forties and fifties earn more.
