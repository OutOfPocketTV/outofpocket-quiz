Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

What we collect

Using the free calculator doesn't require any personal information — your age range, height, income, and other filter choices stay in your browser and are only sent to our server to compute a result.

If you purchase the Global Dream Partner Report, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your email address and payment details at checkout. We never see or store your card number — Stripe handles that directly. We do receive and store your email address and a record of the purchase so we can grant and restore access to your report.

How we use it

  • To unlock and re-open your Global Dream Partner Report on request, without requiring an account or password.
  • To email you a link back to your report (sent via our email provider, Resend) after purchase, or if you ask us to resend it.
  • To keep basic records of purchases for accounting and support purposes.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use your email for marketing unless you separately opt in somewhere else (like following us on social media).

Cookies and local storage

Your browser's local storage remembers whether you've unlocked the report, so you don't have to purchase it again on the same device. This is stored on your device, not shared with us beyond the original purchase record.

This site displays ads through Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad preferences, in Google's own privacy policy.

We also use Vercel's privacy-friendly analytics to understand overall traffic to the site (e.g. how many people visit, which pages are popular). This doesn't use tracking cookies or collect personal information.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers we use to run this site: Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery), and our hosting/database providers (Vercel and Neon). Each only receives what they need to do their job — for example, Resend only sees the email address and access link needed to send you a message.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email address and purchase record at any time by emailing outofpocketinterviews@gmail.com. Note that this will also remove your ability to restore access to a previously purchased report.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email outofpocketinterviews@gmail.com.
