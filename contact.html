Contact

Out Of Pocket TV is one person, so every message lands with the person who films the interviews and built the calculator. Questions, corrections and business enquiries all go to the same place.

Send a message

Or email directly

If you'd rather use your own email, write to outofpocketinterviews@gmail.com. Replies usually take a few days; the channel is a one-person operation and filming days are long.

About a purchase

Lost your report link? On the calculator page, use “Restore access” under the start button and enter the email you paid with; the link is sent again. For refunds, read the refund policy first, then email with the address you used at checkout.

Business and press

Sponsorships, licensing of interview clips, data requests and press questions are welcome. Say who you are, what you have in mind, and any deadline.
