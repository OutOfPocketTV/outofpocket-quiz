About Out Of Pocket TV

Out Of Pocket TV is a street-interview channel. We ask people what they want in a partner — how tall, how much they earn, what age — and then check how many people actually fit.

The calculator

The odds calculator started as a way to settle those conversations with numbers instead of guesses. It takes the standards you set and estimates how many real adults meet all of them, using public data from the U.S. Census Bureau, the CDC, Gallup and Pew Research, plus international sources covering 198 countries. You can read exactly how it works, including where its estimates are weakest.

Who’s behind it

Out Of Pocket TV is one person, not a company. Tom films the interviews, built the calculator, and runs the channel and this site.

How it’s paid for

Answering the questions is free. Seeing your full results is a one-time payment — US$0.99 in the United States, with a local price in other currencies — which covers the report across all 198 countries. There’s no subscription and no account. Some pages may also show ads.
