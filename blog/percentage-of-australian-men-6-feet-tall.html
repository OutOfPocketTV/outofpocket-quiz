What percentage of men in Australia are 6 feet tall?

Out Of Pocket TV · November 6, 2026

About 1 in 4 men in Australia is 6 feet (183 cm) or taller: 26.7% of men aged 18 to 80, or roughly 2.5 million men. That’s a bigger share than in the U.S., the UK or Canada.

What stands out

  • The typical Australian man is about 5′10″ (178 cm). 53% of Australian men are 5′10″ or taller, so a 5′10″ minimum rules out a little under half.
  • From 6 feet up, each inch cuts the pool by about 40%, then more. About 1 Australian man in 4 is 6′0″, 1 in 11 is 6′2″, about 1 in 44 is 6′4″ and about 1 in 110 is 6′5″.
  • Australian men are taller than Americans, even young ones. 26.7% of Australian men are 6 feet or taller, against 18.8% of American men under 45: about 40% more likely.
  • The tallest country outside Europe. Australia comes 15th of the 177 countries with their own data, and first outside Europe, ahead of New Zealand at 22.7%.
  • Australian women are taller too. About 1 woman in 12 is 5′8″ or taller, against about 1 in 16 in the UK. About 1 in 58 is 5′10″ and about 1 in 460 is 6 feet.

How these numbers are worked out

The Australian figures are estimates, not a count of measured men. The calculator takes an average height and spread for Australian adults (an average of about 5′10″ for men and 5′4″ for women), drawn from Australian Bureau of Statistics and WHO data, and fits a bell curve, the same way it treats every country outside the U.S. That curve is the same at every age, so it can’t show that younger men tend to be taller, and the tallest rows are the least certain. The U.S. figures come from the CDC’s measured health survey (NHANES 2021–2023) for each age group, which is why the fair comparison is with American men under 45. Countries close together in the table are effectively tied. Centimeters are rounded. For the UK, see what percentage of men in the UK are 6 feet tall, and for every country, where the tall men are.
