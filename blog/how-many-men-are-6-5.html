How many men are 6′5″ or taller?

Out Of Pocket TV · October 30, 2026

About 1 in 240 American men is 6′5″ or taller: 0.42% of men aged 18 to 80, or roughly 520,000 men in the whole country. Narrow it to unmarried men aged 25 to 35 and it’s about 79,000.

6′5″ became the internet’s shorthand for the impossible standard after a 2024 song went viral asking for a man in finance, with a trust fund, 6′5″ and blue eyes. We can’t count trust funds, jobs or eye color: no national survey records them alongside height. But we can count the height, the income and the rest.

What stands out

  • Above 6 feet, each inch cuts the pool by about half, and by more the taller you go. 6′0″ is about 1 man in 6, 6′2″ about 1 in 21, 6′4″ about 1 in 97 and 6′5″ about 1 in 240.
  • A 6′5″ man is rarer than a 5′10″ woman. About 1 woman in 100 is 5′10″ or taller. A 6′5″ man is closer to a 5′11″ woman, at about 1 in 280.
  • Add six figures and the young, unmarried 6′5″ pool falls to about 11,000. That’s about 1 unmarried man aged 25 to 35 in 2,400.
  • At $250,000 it’s fewer than 1,000 men in the country. About 900, or 1 in 29,000. With no kids as well, about 700. That is before eye color, the job title or the trust fund.

How these numbers are worked out

Height comes from the CDC’s National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (2021–2023), which measures people rather than asking them. Very few men in the sample are 6′5″ or taller, too few to count directly, so these figures come from the bell curve the calculator fits to each age group’s measured heights. That makes them estimates, and the tallest rows are the least certain: the measured count on our height odds page puts 6′4″ at about 1 in 110, a little rarer than the curve’s 1 in 97. Age, marital status and income are counted together from the Census Bureau’s Current Population Survey (March 2025); height is applied on top as if unrelated to income, and taller men do earn slightly more on average. “Not married” includes divorced, widowed and separated men, and isn’t the same as single.
