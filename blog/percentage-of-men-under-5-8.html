What percentage of men are under 5′8″?

Out Of Pocket TV · November 16, 2026

About 3 in 8 American men are under 5′8″: 37.7% of men aged 18 to 80, or roughly 47 million men. About half are under 5′9″, and nearly 2 in 3 are under 5′10″.

Our other height posts start at the tall end. This one starts at the other, which is a lot more crowded: how many men fall under each height, measured rather than self-reported.

What stands out

  • 5′9″ is the middle. About half of American men are under 5′9″ and half are 5′9″ or taller.
  • Under 5′10″ is the majority. About 64% of men are shorter than 5′10″, and 84% are shorter than 6 feet.
  • Younger men are taller. About a third of men under 45 are under 5′8″, against nearly half of men aged 65 to 80.
  • A 5′8″ man is taller than about 95% of women. Even a 5′6″ man is taller than about 4 in 5 women.
  • Under 5′6″ is about 1 man in 6. Under 5′4″ it’s about 1 in 20.

How these numbers are worked out

Height comes from the CDC’s National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES, 2021–2023), which measures people without shoes rather than asking them. The calculator fits a bell curve to each age group’s measured heights; near the middle of the range, where these figures sit, it matches the measured count to within about a percentage point. Each share here is everyone minus the share at or above that height. For the tall end, see height odds and how many men are 6′5″ or taller.
