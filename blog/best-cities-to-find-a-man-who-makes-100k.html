The best U.S. cities to find a man who makes $100k

Out Of Pocket TV · November 13, 2026

In the San Jose metro, about 2 in 5 men earn $100,000 or more a year, against about 1 in 4 across the U.S. San Francisco, Washington and New York round out the top four. By sheer numbers, though, nowhere comes close to New York, with about 2.2 million six-figure men.

Where you live makes a big difference to an income standard. Here are the 40 biggest U.S. metro areas, ranked by the share of men aged 18 to 80 who earn $100,000 or more.

What stands out

  • Silicon Valley is in a league of its own. About 40% of men in the San Jose metro and 37% in the San Francisco metro earn six figures. Around San Jose, about 1 man in 7 earns $200,000 or more, more than twice the national share.
  • Washington, New York, Boston and Seattle come next. Each is around 30% to 33%, so roughly 1 man in 3.
  • New York wins on numbers. About 2.2 million men there earn six figures, nearly twice as many as in the Los Angeles metro, the next biggest.
  • Even the bottom of the list isn’t far below the national rate. San Antonio and Cleveland come last at around 19% to 20%, about 1 man in 5, against 23.6% nationally.
  • A paycheck isn’t the same everywhere. $100,000 goes a lot further in Cleveland than in San Jose, so a six-figure minimum is a stricter standard in some places than others.

How these numbers are worked out

Metro areas are the Census Bureau’s own Metropolitan Statistical Areas, which take in the suburbs, not just the city itself. The national figures are a real count from the Census Bureau’s Current Population Survey (March 2025). For each metro, the calculator adjusts that national count by how the metro’s median personal income (from the Census Bureau’s American Community Survey, rounded) compares with the national median, so the metro figures are estimates. Metros with the same median come out exactly tied. This ranks all men, married or not: marriage rates are only available for metros as a few broad patterns, too rough to rank cities on.
