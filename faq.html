Frequently asked questions

Last updated: September 19, 2026

What is this calculator?

You enter the standards you’d want in a partner — age range, height, income, build, relationship status and more — and it estimates what share of adults meet all of them, using public population data. It was built by Out Of Pocket TV, a street-interview channel that asks people exactly these questions.

Is it based on real data?

Yes. The figures come from the U.S. Census Bureau, the CDC, Gallup and Pew Research for the United States, and from national statistics offices, the UN, the World Health Organization and the World Bank for other countries. It is still an estimate — how the odds are calculated lists every source and the main ways the numbers can be off.

Why is my number so low?

Because standards multiply. If four standards each keep half of people, together they keep one in sixteen — about 6%. A fairly ordinary list gets small fast: a man aged 25 to 35, at least 6 feet tall, earning $100,000 or more and not married works out to about 1.65% of men in that age range, roughly 1 in 60.

What does “Lost in the Matrix” mean?

Every result gets one of five rankings, based on the share of people who match:

RankingShare who match
Local Neighborhood60% or more
Next Town Over30% to 60%
Across the Country10% to 30%
On the Moon2.5% to 10%
Lost in the Matrix2.5% or less

When you search worldwide, “Across the Country” becomes “Across the Globe”. Most people who set several standards land in Lost in the Matrix — which says more about how multiplication works than about anyone’s standards.

Out Of Pocket TV is one person, not a company, and the one-time payment is what keeps the calculator and its data running. It’s US$0.99 in the United States, and visitors in other countries see a price in their own currency. There’s no subscription and no account to create.

On the same browser, your access is remembered automatically. On a new device, or if you’ve cleared your browser, use Restore access at the top of the calculator and enter the email you used at checkout — we’ll send you a link straight back to your report.

Can I get a refund?

Do you store my answers?

Your result is worked out in your browser, so your answers aren’t sent to us to be calculated. We record which filters get used, anonymously, and store an email address only if you buy the report. See the privacy policy.
